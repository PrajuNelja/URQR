Print head dot counts dictate exact character budgets across 80mm and 58mm rolls

ESC/POS thermal receipt printers render strictly monospaced glyphs directly onto a fixed horizontal line of heating elements. Every line budget equals floor(printable_dots / cell_width_dots), which gives standard 203 dpi (8 dots/mm) printers 48 columns on 80mm paper (72mm effective width, 576 dots) and 32 columns on 58mm paper (48mm effective width, 384 dots) in default Font A (12×24 dots) (EDOO, Thermalink Cheat Sheet).

180 dpi hardware variance (42 vs 48 cols)

Popular 80mm units such as the Epson TM-T88V operate at 180 dpi (512 dots), capping Font A at 42 columns rather than 48. The printer daemon must expose a configurable columnsPerLine setting (48 default, 42 fallback, 32 for 58mm) instead of hardcoding 48.

Double-width scaling halves line width

Enabling double-width text via GS ! 0x10 or double-both via GS ! 0x11 cuts 80mm capacity to 24 columns and 58mm to 16 columns (Thermalink Cheat Sheet). Use double-height only (GS ! 0x01) when emphasizing totals without losing horizontal room.

No firmware word wrapping

Printers split lines mid-word the moment the dot buffer overflows, breaking right-aligned amounts (EDOO). Software must wrap item descriptions to the reserved item column budget before transmitting bytes.

Single-byte code pages & ₹ symbol fallback

Budget thermal ROMs expect single-byte tables (PC437, CP850, WPC1252 via ESC t n sent after ESC @) and print ? (0x3F) or boxes for raw UTF-8 ₹ and Indic scripts (ScanPrinter, Karobari). Use ASCII Rs. in raw text mode, or 1-bit raster printing via GS v 0 at 576/384 dots for exact ₹ glyphs and logos.

  • Zone 2 — Order Metadata (Key : Value): Fixed left-aligned label (16 cols), colon at col 17, right-aligned value (31 cols). Includes Bill No., Date, Time, Order Type, and bold Order No. (YOU202611230001). Excludes Table No. and Token No..
  • Zone 3 — Line Items (4-Column Table): ITEM (21 cols left) + QTY (5 cols right) + UNIT (11 cols right) + TOTAL (11 cols right) = 48 cols. Names over 21 characters wrap onto an indented continuation line.
  • Zone 4 — Taxes & Grand Total: Flat 5% tax split evenly into CGST (2.5%) and SGST (2.5%) above a solid-ruled GRAND TOTAL row in bold double-height (ESC E 1 + GS ! 0x01).
  • Zone 5 — Payment Mode: Single row directly under GRAND TOTAL showing Payment Mode (Cash, UPI, or Other). Both Customer Name and Payment Status are removed.
  • Zone 6 — Streamlined Footer & Cut: Centered THANK YOU, VISIT AGAIN! and -- * -- star divider (no redundant second thank-you lines), followed by ESC d 4 feed and GS V 66 0 cut (Thermalink Cheat Sheet).

Monospaced font sizing, line height, and divider dash standards

Standardizing exact dot cells, line feeds, and ASCII rule characters ensures visual parity between browser @media print output and raw ESC/POS bytes emitted by the local printer bridge. Font B is reserved strictly for secondary fine print because 9-dot glyphs lose legibility under dim dining lighting (EDOO).

Every row sum below strictly equals the printer's exact column ceiling so right-aligned prices and colons never wrap. On 58mm (32 columns), the UNIT price moves to an indented second line (2 x 49.00) so item names retain 17 usable characters.
